Business Process Specification

From the CRM Sales Opportunity to Project Closure — the officially agreed business reference, prepared for management review, functional analysis, and future Odoo configuration

Business Process Documentation Agreed reference prior to implementation Ready for approval committee review Sales · Business Development · Operations · Finance · Management

Purpose

This document exists to formally document and standardize the complete operational process governing the journey of a sales opportunity, from its creation in the CRM system through the closure of its associated project, serving as a single official reference for how Sales, Business Development, Operations, Finance, and Management collaborate.

This process unifies the full lifecycle — commercial qualification of the opportunity, field assessment, budget preparation and approval, quotation and sales order issuance, through to project execution and closure — with each phase's owner, inputs, outputs, and handover points to other departments clearly defined.

This document is adopted as the official business reference prior to any configuration or automation work within the Odoo platform, ensuring that any future technical design reflects the agreed operational logic without reinterpretation or deviation.

● Sales ● Business Development ● Operations ● Finance ● Management
Role structure: Sales owns the opportunity — creating and managing it. Business Development is the coordinating role that follows the opportunity through Operations, Finance, and Management until every stage is complete. Operations is the department responsible for the site visit, budget preparation, project creation, task assignment, execution, and closure, and includes several internal teams such as the planning team, among others.

4 Key Decision Points

The points where the process branches based on a business condition or human decision.

Is a Site Visit required?

Determined by Operations based on the nature of the project, before the Budget Builder begins.

Does the Project Manager approve the Budget?

The first approval gate after Operations prepares the budget; rejection sends it back for revision within Operations.

Does Finance approve the Budget?

Finance reviews only the Financial Summary (cost, margin).

Does Management approve the Budget?

The final, decisive approval; only after this does the Budget move to "Approved" status.

Does the customer approve the Quotation?

A revision request sends Sales back to update the quotation; approval moves directly to Sales Order creation.

7 Department User Journeys

What each department does, step by step, from its own perspective.

🔵 Sales

  1. Creates the Opportunity with customer & project details
  2. Tracks progress via the Opportunity Dashboard
  3. Creates the Quotation once the budget is approved
  4. Adds operational costs and sends the quotation to the customer
  5. Follows up on customer approval and creates the Sales Order

🔷 Business Development

  1. Receives the Opportunity from Sales as soon as it's qualified
  2. Coordinates its handover to Operations
  3. Follows the budget through all three approval stages
  4. Ensures the opportunity never stalls at any stage without follow-up

🟠 Operations

  1. Receives the assigned Opportunity
  2. Determines and, if needed, performs the Site Visit
  3. Prepares the full Budget and secures Project Manager approval internally
  4. Creates the Project from a Template once the Sales Order exists
  5. Assigns tasks and executes the project through to closure

🟢 Finance

  1. Receives the Budget after Project Manager approval
  2. Reviews only the Financial Summary
  3. Approves or rejects the budget, stating a clear reason

🟣 Management

  1. Receives the Budget after Finance approval
  2. Conducts the final, comprehensive review
  3. Gives final approval or rejection with remarks
  4. Tracks project performance from high-level dashboards

11 Phase-by-Phase Process Specification

A formal functional specification for each phase of the process: ownership, trigger, inputs, outputs, handover points, and exit criteria — serving as a reference for functional analysis and future Odoo configuration.

Ticketing & SLA Mechanism: To ensure accountability and transparency between departments, a formal ticket is raised for each participating external department at every handover point described below, identifying the Process Owner responsible for delivering that phase, along with a target response time (SLA). The SLA values shown in this section are indicative, proposed as a starting point, and require final sign-off from the relevant department heads before formal adoption.

1 Opportunity Creation & Qualification

Purpose: Establish the starting point of the engagement and capture initial customer and project information in a single, traceable record.

Trigger: A new lead or customer inquiry is identified Primary Department: Sales Supporting Departments: Business Development Process Owner: Sales Representative / Account Manager
Inputs
  • Customer contact information
  • Initial customer requirements
  • Project background
Outputs / Deliverables
  • Qualified CRM Opportunity record
Handover: Opportunity details (customer & project information) are handed over to Business Development.
Exit Criteria: The Opportunity is qualified and ready for coordination with Operations.

2 Business Development Coordination

Purpose: Ensure the Opportunity progresses through Operations, Finance, and Management without stalling, through a single coordination point across departments.

Trigger: Opportunity is qualified by Sales Primary Department: Business Development Supporting Departments: Sales, Operations Process Owner: Business Development Manager
Inputs
  • Qualified Opportunity
  • Customer requirements
Outputs / Deliverables
  • Opportunity routed to Operations
  • Coordination log
Handover: Opportunity details are handed over to Operations.
Exit Criteria: The Opportunity is formally assigned to Operations with access granted.

3 Operations Engagement

Purpose: Establish operational ownership of the Opportunity ahead of feasibility assessment and budget preparation.

Trigger: Opportunity reaches the Operations stage Primary Department: Operations Supporting Departments: Business Development Process Owner: Operations Manager
Inputs
  • Opportunity details
  • Customer requirements
  • Scope of work
Outputs / Deliverables
  • Site Visit requirement decision
Handover: The Site Visit requirement decision is handed over to the Site Visit team or Budget Builder team within Operations.
Exit Criteria: A decision is made on whether a Site Visit is required.

4 Site Visit (Conditional)

Purpose: Validate on-site conditions and gather accurate measurements to support budget preparation.

Trigger: A Site Visit is determined to be required Primary Department: Operations Supporting Departments: — Process Owner: Operations Manager / Site Survey Team
Inputs
  • Opportunity details
  • Site location
  • Scope of work
Outputs / Deliverables
  • Site Survey Report (survey, measurements, notes, photos, attachments)
Handover: The Site Survey Report is handed over to the Budget Builder preparation step.
Exit Criteria: The Site Visit is completed and fully documented.

5 Budget Builder Preparation

Purpose: Consolidate all cost elements (purchases, expenses, manpower) into a single structured budget with an automatic financial summary.

Trigger: Site Visit completed (if applicable), or directly after Opportunity assignment Primary Department: Operations Supporting Departments: — Process Owner: Operations Manager / Budget Preparer
Inputs
  • Site Survey Report (if applicable)
  • Customer requirements & scope of work
  • Vendor pricing
Outputs / Deliverables
  • Purchase Requirements
  • Expense Plan
  • Manpower Plan
  • Financial Summary
Handover: The full Budget is handed over to the Project Manager for approval.
Exit Criteria: The Budget is formally submitted for approval.

6 Project Manager Approval

Purpose: Confirm the operational accuracy and completeness of the Budget before it is reviewed financially.

Trigger: Budget submitted Primary Department: Operations Supporting Departments: — Process Owner: Project Manager
Inputs
  • Full Budget
  • Financial Summary
Outputs / Deliverables
  • Project Manager-approved Budget
Handover: The approved Budget is handed over to Finance.
Exit Criteria: The Budget is approved by the Project Manager.

7 Finance Approval

Purpose: Validate the financial soundness of the Budget, focused exclusively on cost and margin.

Trigger: Project Manager approval granted Primary Department: Finance Supporting Departments: — Process Owner: Finance Manager
Inputs
  • Project Manager-approved Budget
  • Financial Summary
Outputs / Deliverables
  • Finance-approved Budget
Handover: The Finance-approved Budget is handed over to Management.
Exit Criteria: The Budget is approved by Finance.

8 Management Approval

Purpose: Provide the final organizational sign-off on the Budget before the quotation is issued.

Trigger: Finance approval granted Primary Department: Management Supporting Departments: — Process Owner: Senior Management
Inputs
  • Finance-approved Budget
  • Financial Summary
Outputs / Deliverables
  • Final Approved Budget
Handover: The Approved Budget is handed over to Sales.
Exit Criteria: The Budget status changes to "Approved".

9 Quotation Generation

Purpose: Translate the approved Budget into a formal commercial offer for the customer.

Trigger: Budget approved Primary Department: Sales Supporting Departments: — Process Owner: Sales Representative / Account Manager
Inputs
  • Approved Budget
  • Financial Summary reference
  • Commercial terms
Outputs / Deliverables
  • Customer Quotation
Handover: The Quotation is handed over to the customer (external).
Exit Criteria: The Quotation is issued and sent to the customer.

10 Customer Approval

Purpose: Obtain the customer's formal acceptance of the commercial proposal.

Trigger: Quotation sent to the customer Primary Department: Sales Supporting Departments: — Process Owner: Sales Representative / Account Manager
Inputs
  • Quotation
  • Customer feedback
Outputs / Deliverables
  • Customer decision (Approved / Revision Requested)
Handover: Customer approval confirmation is handed over to Sales (Order Creation).
Exit Criteria: Customer approval is received.

11 Sales Order Creation

Purpose: Formalize the commercial agreement internally and initiate the transition to execution.

Trigger: Customer approval received Primary Department: Sales Supporting Departments: — Process Owner: Sales Representative / Account Manager
Inputs
  • Customer-approved Quotation
  • Customer confirmation
Outputs / Deliverables
  • Confirmed Sales Order
Handover: The Sales Order, Approved Budget, and Customer Requirements are handed over to Operations.
Exit Criteria: The Sales Order is confirmed.

12 Project Creation

Purpose: Establish the execution workspace required to deliver the awarded project.

Trigger: Sales Order confirmed Primary Department: Operations Supporting Departments: — Process Owner: Operations Manager / Project Manager
Inputs
  • Sales Order
  • Approved Budget
  • Project Template
Outputs / Deliverables
  • Project record linked to the Opportunity
Handover: Project details are handed over to the execution team.
Exit Criteria: The Project is created and linked to the Opportunity.

13 Task Assignment

Purpose: Distribute execution responsibilities across the delivery team.

Trigger: Project created Primary Department: Operations Supporting Departments: — Process Owner: Project Manager
Inputs
  • Project Template tasks
  • Manpower Plan
Outputs / Deliverables
  • Assigned task list
Handover: Task assignments are handed over to the execution team.
Exit Criteria: All initial tasks are assigned.

14 Project Execution

Purpose: Deliver the project scope while maintaining clear progress visibility for all stakeholders.

Trigger: Tasks assigned Primary Department: Operations Supporting Departments: Sales (monitoring), Management (monitoring) Process Owner: Project Manager
Inputs
  • Assigned tasks
  • Project Template
  • Manpower Plan
Outputs / Deliverables
  • Updated task status
  • Project Progress data
Handover: Progress updates are handed over to the Opportunity Dashboard (visible to Sales, Business Development, and Management).
Exit Criteria: All project tasks are completed.

15 Project Closure

Purpose: Formally conclude the project and confirm that delivery is complete.

Trigger: All project tasks completed Primary Department: Operations Supporting Departments: Management Process Owner: Project Manager / Operations Manager
Inputs
  • Completed tasks
  • Final deliverables
Outputs / Deliverables
  • Project Completion confirmation
Handover: Project Completion status is handed over to the Opportunity record (final status update).
Exit Criteria: The Project is marked as Closed.

12 Points for Discussion & Potential Integration into the Process

Items presented to the departments for joint discussion, with an initial proposal for how each could be integrated into the process phases documented above (Section 11), once agreed.

1

Set an SLA for each approval stage

For example: 24 hours for the Project Manager, 48 hours for Finance, 48 hours for Management, with automatic alerts when a deadline is exceeded.

✅ Already integrated: Section 11 includes a "Ticket & SLA" field for every phase — only final sign-off on the values from department heads is needed.
2

Require a documented reason for every rejection

This avoids repeated revision cycles without a clear understanding of what's actually needed.

↳ Proposed integration: add a mandatory "Rejection Reason" field to Phases 6, 7, and 8 (Project Manager / Finance / Management Approval) in Section 11, linked to the approval flow in Section 5.
3

A single clear owner for the "Is a Site Visit required?" decision

Designating the Operations Manager as the sole decision owner avoids inconsistent, ad-hoc judgment calls.

↳ Proposed integration: explicitly confirm the "Operations Manager" as the decision owner in Phase 3 (Operations Engagement) and its corresponding decision point in Section 4.
4

Standardized project templates, reviewed periodically

Periodic review of project templates ensures they reflect the actual types of projects being delivered.

↳ Proposed integration: add as a periodic governance task linked to Phase 12 (Project Creation), without altering the process sequence itself.
5

A cycle-time KPI for the full process

Measuring the time from opportunity creation to project closure as a comprehensive key performance indicator.

↳ Proposed integration: add a "Full Cycle Time" field to the Opportunity Dashboard in Section 9, calculated from the dates of Phase 1 and Phase 15.
6

Track customer response time on the quotation

Measuring the time between sending the quotation and receiving customer approval helps identify opportunities to speed up this stage.

↳ Proposed integration: directly tied to Phase 10 (Customer Approval) in Section 11, where this measurement can be shown as part of its SLA tracking.
7

One documented handover point between Business Development and Operations

A clear handover checklist when the opportunity moves from Business Development to Operations ensures all required data is complete.

↳ Proposed integration: add as a checklist attached to the handover point in Phase 2 (Business Development Coordination) in Section 11.
