Maps — RFI / RFP / RFQ

RFI · RFP · RFQ System-builder perspective (Odoo)
Methodology note: The three maps below (Sections 1, 2, 3) are a direct visual representation of exactly what is written in the three uploaded files — the departments, activities, handover points, and sequence, as documented, with nothing added, removed, or reordered. Section 4 (Gaps) is entirely separate from the maps and changes nothing in them; it simply flags points left undecided in the original text, from the perspective of someone who will build this process as an actual system (Odoo), so they can be discussed and resolved before technical design begins.

4 Gaps Observed from a System-Builder Perspective

Original observations from a system-builder perspective, with notes from the discussion with Bandar added beneath each item.

Gaps common to all three files (RFI / RFP / RFQ)

Undocumented path
The "Decline to participate" case is not defined

All three files mention a "should we participate?" decision, but only document the "Yes" path. There is no description of what happens if the decision is "No" — no closure status for the opportunity, no client notification, no corresponding system state. This needs to be resolved, since any system needs a path for every outcome, not just the positive one.

Discussion Outcome
(Based on the discussion with Mr. Bandar)

The participation decision will be handled during the Lead Qualification stage, which precedes Opportunity creation.

Only qualified Leads will be converted into Opportunities.

Requests that are not pursued will remain at the Lead stage and will not proceed through this workflow.
Undefined criteria
No clear criteria for "does the request fall within Peak's services?" or "commercial viability"

This decision is left to Business Development's judgment with no documented criteria (e.g., a minimum opportunity size, or service type). From a system perspective, this means the field will remain free-text/judgment-based and not automatable or quantitatively reportable unless a criterion is defined later.

Discussion Outcome
(Based on the discussion with Mr. Bandar)

The qualification decision will be performed during Lead Qualification.

Business qualification criteria (customer targets, sales targets, strategic objectives, etc.) will be defined separately and may evolve over time.
No SLA
No turnaround time (SLA) exists for any phase across all three files

There is no mention of an expected response time for any activity — not for HR/Finance document provision, not for Planning's review, not for Finance's approval of the quotation. While the "client's final submission deadline" is mentioned as a deliverable, there is no internal tracking mechanism to ensure it isn't missed.

Discussion Outcome
(Based on the discussion with Mr. Bandar)

Service Level Agreements (SLAs) for requests and activities will be defined separately by Mr. Bandar based on business requirements.
Incomplete definition
The entities "Parkit", "ImagineX", and "Hemmtk" are not defined in context

Question to resolve: Will they be treated as vendors (pricing via Procurement only), or will tasks be assigned to them as internal departments within the workflow?

Discussion Outcome
(Based on the discussion with Mr. Bandar)

These companies will be treated as external vendors/service providers only.

They are not internal departments.

Pricing and commercial engagement will be handled through Procurement.
Undocumented
No documented path for a failed documentation completeness check

"Business Development verifies all required documentation has been received before submission" — but there is no description of what happens if it's incomplete (escalation? delaying submission? alerting the department that is behind?).

Discussion Outcome
(Based on the discussion with Mr. Bandar)

Business Development is responsible for following up with the relevant departments to obtain any missing documents before submission.
Undocumented
No limit on the number of clarification rounds with the client

Across all three files, the "clarification request → client response → update" cycle is documented as a single step, but nothing indicates whether this cycle can repeat multiple times, what the maximum is, or at what point the team should stop seeking clarification and proceed with what's available.

Discussion Outcome
(Based on the discussion with Mr. Bandar)

The clarification cycle remains open during this phase.

Business Development will continue communicating with the customer until enough information is available.
Unclear relationship
No documented link between these three processes and the "Opportunity → Project" process documented earlier

All three files end at "submit the response/proposal to the client," while the other process we documented earlier (from budget approval through project closure) begins roughly at the point of customer approval of the quotation. There is no text clarifying the exact meeting point between them: is "Customer Approval" here the same as "Customer Approval" there? Is "register the opportunity in the system" in Phase 1 here the same Opportunity record referenced there?

Discussion Outcome
(Based on the discussion with Mr. Bandar)

Add a clear reference indicating that once the customer approves the proposal, the process continues using the existing Opportunity to Project workflow to complete the end-to-end business process.
Undocumented
No mention of a document repository/storage system (NDA, corporate documents, technical proposals)

"Filing" is mentioned as an activity (e.g., "Executed NDA is filed") without specifying where or how — important for designing document management (DMS) within or outside Odoo.

Discussion Outcome
(Based on the discussion with Mr. Bandar)

Project documents may either:

• be stored inside the customer document folder using project tags

OR

• be linked directly to the Project record.

The customer record should provide access to all related project documents.
Not specified
The intake mechanism ("Peak Info email") is not specified technically

Question to resolve: Will the mailbox be technically integrated with the system (automatic Lead Capture), or will entry remain manual by Business Development?

Discussion Outcome
(Based on the discussion with Mr. Bandar)

Requests will be entered manually by Business Development.

No automatic email integration is planned.
Question to clarify
Can a request's classification change mid-process? (e.g., an RFI evolving into an RFP)

The three files are treated as entirely separate. The only question raised here for discussion: is this scenario actually possible in practice, or not?

Discussion Outcome
(Based on the discussion with Mr. Bandar)

Business confirmation is required to determine:

- whether request type may change during execution

and

- whether these request types share one technical workflow with different request types or independent workflows.

Gaps specific to RFI

No items — the previously noted item about internal review was removed per Bandar's direction.

Gaps specific to RFP

Unresolved
No senior Management approval at any point

The only approval mentioned for the commercial proposal is Finance's review of profitability and margin. There is no mention of any higher management sign-off before the proposal is submitted to the client, despite this existing as a third approval layer in the later budget process (after project award) documented previously.

Discussion Outcome
(Based on the discussion with Mr. Bandar)

A Management Approval step has been added to the RFP workflow after Finance Approval and before proposal submission to the client.
Sequence not fully clear
The relationship between Planning and Operations in Phase 4 (Technical Kick-Off) is not defined with enough precision

The text states that Business Development distributes the package to Planning, and that Operations assigns a Project Manager — but it's unclear whether this assignment happens in parallel with Planning's review or after an explicit request from Planning.

Gaps specific to RFQ

Unresolved
No senior Management approval here either

Same observation as RFP: the only approval on the commercial proposal is from Finance.

Discussion Outcome
(Based on the discussion with Mr. Bandar)

A Management Approval step has been added to the RFQ workflow after Finance Approval and before proposal submission to the client.
Overlap not clarified
Significant overlap between RFQ Phases 1-3 and RFP/RFI Phases 1-3

The first three phases (receipt & qualification, NDA management, corporate documentation collection) are nearly identical in wording across all three files. It's not clarified whether this means they are, in practice, the exact same technical path in the system (same fields/record types) regardless of request type, or entirely separate paths for each type.

Discussion Outcome
(Based on the discussion with Mr. Bandar)

Business confirmation is required to determine:

- whether request type may change during execution

and

- whether these request types share one technical workflow with different request types or independent workflows.
