4 Points Clarified & Approved with Management

The following points were previously raised as gaps and have since been clarified and approved following the meeting with Mr. Bandar — except for the SLA item, and one additional item (flagged below), which remain pending.

Points common to all three files (RFI / RFP / RFQ) — Resolved

Resolved
The "Decline to participate" case

Decision: The business process starts from the Lead stage, not the Opportunity stage. The participation decision is made during Lead Qualification; only qualified Leads are converted into Opportunities, while unqualified Leads remain at the Lead stage with the reason for disqualification documented.

Resolved
Criteria for "does the request fall within Peak's services?" or "commercial viability"

Decision: The qualification decision is performed during Lead Qualification. Business qualification criteria (customer targets, sales targets, strategic objectives, etc.) will be defined separately and may evolve over time.

Resolved
The entities "Parkit", "ImagineX", and "Hemmtk"

Decision: These companies will be treated as external vendors/service providers only, not internal departments. Pricing and commercial engagement with them will be handled through Procurement.

Resolved
Failed documentation completeness check before submission

Decision: Business Development is responsible for following up with the relevant departments to obtain any missing documents before submission.

Resolved
Limit on the number of clarification rounds with the client

Decision: The clarification cycle remains intentionally open during this phase, with Business Development continuing to communicate with the customer until enough information is available — no maximum number of rounds is set.

Resolved
Link between these three processes and the "Opportunity → Project" process

Decision: Once the customer approves the proposal, the process continues using the existing Opportunity-to-Project workflow to complete the end-to-end business process.

Resolved
Document repository/storage system (NDA, corporate documents, technical proposals)

Decision: Project documents may either be stored inside the customer document folder using project tags, or linked directly to the Project record. In either case, the customer record must provide access to all documents from its related projects.

Resolved
Intake mechanism ("Peak Info email")

Decision: Requests will be entered manually by Business Development. No automatic email integration is planned at this time.

Points specific to RFP

Resolved
Senior Management approval

Decision: A Management Approval step has been added to the RFP workflow immediately after Finance Approval and before proposal submission to the client — the RFP diagram above (Section 2) has been updated to reflect this.

Not yet resolved
The relationship between Planning and Operations in Phase 4 (Technical Kick-Off)

It's still not defined with enough precision whether the Project Manager assignment by Operations happens in parallel with Planning's review or after an explicit request from Planning. This item was not raised in the latest meeting and still needs to be resolved.

Points specific to RFQ

Resolved
Senior Management approval

Decision: A Management Approval step has been added to the RFQ workflow immediately after Finance Approval and before proposal submission to the client — the RFQ diagram above (Section 3) has been updated to reflect this.

Resolved
Overlap between RFQ Phases 1-3 and RFP/RFI Phases 1-3, and whether a request's classification can change mid-process

Decision: Further confirmation from Management is required to determine whether these request types actually share one technical workflow or are entirely independent — to be resolved later.

Open item — pending resolution

Not yet resolved
No turnaround time (SLA) exists for any phase across all three files

Only the principle has been agreed: Service Level Agreements (SLAs) for requests and activities will be defined by the relevant departments according to business requirements — but no actual values have been set yet.
